# Source validation boundary

The demonstration was generated rather than independently extracted from CIHI hospital indicators. Its province-level values, diagnosis counts, COVID trajectories and 10% cost scenario are **not validated official results**.

Official research starting point: https://www.cihi.ca/en/quick-stats

To produce an official report, record the exact CIHI table and download date, the readmission definition, diagnosis grouping, years, numerator and denominator, risk adjustment, and geography. Reproduce each chart from that export. A readmission rate alone does not establish which events were preventable or what an intervention would save.

**Validation status:** Methodology demonstration only. No observed hospital readmission rates have been independently reproduced from official files in this repository.
